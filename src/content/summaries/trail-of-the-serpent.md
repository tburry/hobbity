With the temple cleared, its priest dead, and the Golden Grain Inn a smoking ruin, the Toad Stompers turn from the village to the cult's road out of it. Ramne the old druid points them northwest—past the Dimwood, into a swamp called the Rushmoors—and lends them his snake-killing weasel. Beneath the burnt inn they find the entrance: a hot, low, hand-dug troglodyte tunnel that has been spiriting families out of Orlane for months. A mile of it brings them up into a ruined courtyard in the woods, where a hobbit from Gomwick stands chained to a stake with a lizard baited at him. Beyond that courtyard the trail goes cold, and the Dimwood has no intention of letting anyone through it. It gives up on the third morning and puts them out in wheat country a mile north of Orlane, where troglodytes drive a bear into their camp and are cut down for it. The trail picks up again in the Rushmoors, in a trodden path through the mud: hobbit feet, goblin feet, and froglike feet, all walking northwest together. At the end of it stands a diked island with the marsh held back and the ground dug three levels down, torchlight in the pit, and a green-painted marble Explictica standing half-roped where the ropes gave out. The cult is not building a temple out in the swamp. It is digging one up.
